Refuge for Sinners

Have you strayed from God? Are you carrying hidden struggles, shame, or regret? Does God feel distant or do you feel disconnected from the church community?

You are not alone. Join a sisterhood of women who have wandered but are ready to come home. Together, we create a judgement free and welcoming space to experience God’s love firsthand and to build a personal, life-giving relationship with God our Father.

Prodigal Sisterhood is a refuge and healing space for women who know, deep within, that they need restoration and are ready to take a BOLD step forward. It is for women who feel lost, outcast, or burdened by their past. This is an inclusive and compassionate environment rooted in grace; however, judgment of any member will not be tolerated and will result in removal from our program.

Building Community

ProdiGals United is a faith-based nonprofit founded in 2025 to foster Christ-centered sisterhood and healing within local communities. Our stories typically connect to other lost sheep that can’t find light within the Church to do life alongside. We create an environment where women can come together to seek and encounter a God who loves them. We learn about the God that created us and the Brother he sent to save us from the trenches we find ourselves entangled in.

Support & Healing

ProdiGals strive for every ProdiGAL to experience the true love of God by:

  • Hearing the inner child within you
  • Embracing and empathizing with the girl you once were
  • Advocating for the woman you are becoming
  • Living fully as the forgiven you
  • Intentionally inviting God into every part of your life
  • Navigating the unknown by faith
  • Grounding ourselves in God word
